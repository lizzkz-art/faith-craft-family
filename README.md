# Faith Craft Family Edition

A free, offline, block-building Bible adventure for ages 3 to 14 that practices reading, vocabulary, and speech skills. Not a medical or therapy product. No ads, no chat, no accounts, no data collection.

- **Play:** https://lizzkz-art.github.io/faith-craft-family/play/
- **About:** https://lizzkz-art.github.io/faith-craft-family/

Features: up to 6 player profiles per device, Pre-K to Grade 8 reading and vocabulary levels, a short adaptive placement quiz (listening-only for ages 3–4), a pre-reader mode, a PIN-protected parent area with report cards, and an optional "Follow school order (MCPS)" setting. Sources for the school order are in [CURRICULUM_SOURCES.md](CURRICULUM_SOURCES.md).

The placement quiz is a quick starting-point estimate, not a formal reading assessment.

Made for iPad. Open the Play link in Safari, then tap Share > **Add to Home Screen** so it opens full screen and works offline after the first load. Progress is saved only on the device.

Bible text: World English Bible (British Edition), public domain. Voices: Kokoro-82M (Apache 2.0). Fonts: Lexend and OpenDyslexic (SIL Open Font License). 3D engine: three.js (MIT).
